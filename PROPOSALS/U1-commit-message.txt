Add Stage 0 validation harness; fidelity root cause found; live smoke test FAILED

New test-only replay harness (FixtureReplayHarness.swift) and diagnostic
tests (AudioFixtureReplayTests.swift) validate the three saved fixtures
through the corrected recognition/timing path, sharing a new
SpeechTranscriptTimingMapper.swift with the live mic path. A
ten-experiment investigation (E1-E10, independently reviewed twice)
root-caused the on-device fidelity-floor failure to
SFSpeechRecognitionResult's replacement-not-cumulative semantics across
internal utterance boundaries on this device/OS/config -- not primarily
signal level, feed mechanism, or session category, all tested and ruled
out. No fix is included; a test-only accumulation prototype is deferred
to its own unit (U5).

The live on-device smoke test (rollover + interruption) FAILED. A
chained interruption (Observatory -> Siri -> Maps -> manual close ->
Observatory) left speech recognition silently dead for the rest of a
~2m30s reflection. Forensic verification of a device backup found two
further defects: (1) audioRecordingReliable/missingAudioIntervals did not
record the disruption, so the D-09 reliability gate would pass
contaminated data downstream; (2) an orphaned header-only recording was
never finalized on the crash/abandon path. Neither is fixed here --
scoped as a new unit (U6, ahead of U5), requiring its own proposal and
review before any change to SpeechRecognitionManager.swift.

This commit is a checkpoint of validated findings, not working
transcription: the fidelity floor is not met and the interruption/
reliability defects are unresolved. See PLAN.md and
STABILIZATION_AND_EXPRESSION_PLAN.md for full detail and the open-unit
queue (U5, U6).
