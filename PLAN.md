4. **E8 — raw callback/aggregation audit: DONE (2026-10-22). ROOT CAUSE FOUND.** `replay(frameRange:onCallback:)` logged every callback (partial+final) off raw `result.bestTranscription`, before any of our own mapping. Finding: within one continuous ~55s request, `segmentCount`/`transcriptLength` repeatedly **drop mid-stream** (segmentCount decreasing between consecutive callbacks). Hermes recount from the raw bundle log (`Test-Observatory-2026.10.22_19-59-35`, zstd-extracted): segmentCount drops in 30/318 callbacks (multi-a), 33/336 (multi-b), 21/345 (solo); counting any segmentCount-or-transcriptLength drop: 39/45/27 — identical pattern in all 3 trials per fixture. Caveats: small count-drops may be ordinary hypothesis revisions rather than utterance resets (only the large resets, e.g. 211→4 chars, are unambiguous); "every completed utterance appears intact in a pre-reset timestamped partial" is OBSERVED, not guaranteed; a similar disappear-after-pause behavior is anecdotally reported on Apple's developer forums (thread 762952) — corroboration, not a guarantee.
5. **E9 — short-passage control: DONE (2026-10-22). Confirms E8's mechanism.** A single clearly-audible 12s passage (`fixture-multi-b-loudnorm` 36–48s; `fixture-solo-loudnorm` 38–50s as secondary) recovers **fully** regardless of position when padded with silence (not competing speech): multi-b alone=28, start=29, end=37 words (vs. 28 in isolation); solo alone=20, start=20, end=18. Duration/position alone don't cause loss — loss only happens when *new speech* follows a pause (triggering E8's reset), confirmed by this negative control's clean recovery. Caveats (per ChatGPT review #2): "full recovery" is by word COUNT only, not alignment-verified — the same 12s passage yielding 28 (alone) vs 37 (end-position) is unexplained and could include insertions/repetitions; and the "new speech after a pause" trigger is inferred, not isolated (speech–pause–speech and continuous-speech controls with varied pause lengths were not run).
6. **E10 — controls & metric hygiene: DONE (2026-10-22).** (a) identity re-encode (no loudnorm) matches E3's baseline almost exactly (28/288, 29/192, 28/149 vs. E3's 27/288, 29/192, 28/149) — re-encoding itself is a non-factor.
